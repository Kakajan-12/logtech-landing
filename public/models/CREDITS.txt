truck.glb — DAF XF 2013 tractor with trailer, converted from the source model kept
locally in /Truck (not committed). Cab interior and engine removed, geometry
simplified and compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression), textures resized to 2048/512 px.

Repainted in Hebent colours (gradient #0F833F -> #08945A): the trailer artwork, the
cab paint and decor stripes are replaced, XF badges removed, and the raised DAF
letters on the grille are replaced by a Hebent Fleet badge plate.
